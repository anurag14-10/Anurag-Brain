# Source Semantics — Anurag-Brain

How source systems are interpreted specifically in the context of the
**AI Diffusion Pathway Framework**.

---

## `transcript`
Interview transcripts are the **primary raw source** for micro-innovations and Failure & Fix units.
- Practitioners speak openly about what broke, vendor issues, and institutional pushback.
- When extracting from transcripts: look for **the workaround** — the workaround reveals the real baseline.
- Look for the moment an institutional champion took a personal risk.

## `document`
Project documentation and technical design docs provide the **formal scaffold**:
- Architecture diagrams establish the component boundaries.
- SLA sheets confirm whether data dependencies were formalised or informal.
- Caution: Documents tend to report the planned system rather than what survived pilot contact.

## `conversation`
Interactive chat captures **adopter inquiries and rapid gap filling**:
- Used during Adopter Guidance to identify the user's current Stage and Dimension.
- Used to probe: "Have you named your data owners yet?"

## `public`
Public sources provide external corroboration of scale and public reception.

---

## Fields requiring explicit human sign-off
- `applies_when` and `fails_when` condition boundaries.
- Attribution of failure modes to named partners.
